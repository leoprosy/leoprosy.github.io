Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Satisfy is a personal tool that publishes the author's own videos to the author's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no users other than the author.

Data accessed

  • TikTok: the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish. They are used only to publish videos to the connected account and to check their publishing status.
  • YouTube: the scope youtube.upload. It is used only to upload videos.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only in a local configuration file on the author's computer. The tool has no server or database and no analytics or tracking. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Third parties

Videos and their metadata are sent only to TikTok and YouTube through their official APIs. Each platform's own privacy policy applies to that data.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings. Deleting the local configuration file removes all stored tokens.
